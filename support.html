Common questions

How do I share a dog with my family?
Open the dog, tap the share button at the top, choose "Can make changes", and send the invite through Messages or Mail. To use WhatsApp or another app, choose Copy Link and enter the person's Apple Account email or phone number first. They tap the link and the dog appears in their DogMinder.

A shared dog isn't showing up.
Make sure both phones are signed in to iCloud and DogMinder is turned on under Settings → your name → iCloud. In DogMinder, Settings → Sync shows whether uploads and downloads are working, and any error.

Reminders aren't going off.
Allow notifications for DogMinder in iPhone Settings, and check that "Mute reminders on this phone" is off in DogMinder's Settings.

Does DogMinder cost anything?
No. It's free, with no ads or in-app purchases. Data is stored in your own iCloud storage.
