Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

DogMinder is built so that your information stays yours. In short: we don't collect, see or sell any of your data.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter, including dog profiles, photos, health records, vaccinations, reminders and expenses, is stored on your device and in your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. The developer has no servers and cannot access your iCloud data.

Sharing with family

When you share a dog, the people you invite can see and, depending on the permission you choose, edit that dog's records. Sharing is handled by Apple's iCloud. You can see who has access, change permissions or stop sharing at any time from the dog's share button. The name you enter in DogMinder's Settings is shown to people you share with, next to items you log.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in beyond your existing Apple Account and iCloud.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No collecting, selling or sharing of personal data with anyone.

Notifications and photos

Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device. Photos you choose for a dog's profile are stored with that dog's record in iCloud. DogMinder only accesses the photos you pick.

Deleting your data

Deleting a dog in the app removes it and its records from your iCloud and from everyone it was shared with. You can also remove all DogMinder data from iCloud in iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children

DogMinder doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
