Your dog's records and reminders, shared with your family.

DogMinder keeps your dog's profile, health log, vaccinations, reminders and expenses in one place, and syncs them through iCloud to everyone you share with.

  • Profiles: breed, birthday, adoption day, weight, microchip, vet contact and notes.
  • Health log and vaccines: vet visits, medications and grooming, with up-to-date, due-soon and overdue vaccine status.
  • Reminders: medicine, meals, walks and vet appointments, on every family member's phone, with who did what.
  • Expenses: food, vet bills, toys and more, with monthly and yearly totals.
  • Family sharing: invite family members through iCloud. Everyone can view and edit, and changes sync automatically.
